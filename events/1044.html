How is this computed?

Computed from the scorecards of the league phase: 2 points for a win, 1 for a tie or no result. Bonus points, penalties and rain-revised targets are not applied, and matches abandoned without a ball bowled are not in the data, so official tables may differ slightly. NRR uses full quota overs for all-out innings.
