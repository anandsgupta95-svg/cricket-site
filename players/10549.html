Manav Jagdusakumar Suthar (born (2002-08-03)3 August 2002) is an Indian cricketer who plays for the Indian national team. He is a bowling all-rounder, who bats left-handed and bowls slow left-arm orthodox spin. He represents Rajasthan in domestic cricket and Gujarat Titans in the Indian Premier League.
