Daljeet Singh (born 14 October 1995) is a cricketer who plays for the Hong Kong national cricket team. Daljeet is a right-handed batsman and bowls right-arm medium. Daljeet made his Twenty20 cricket debut against Nepal national cricket team on 13 March 2012. He played a total of four T20 games.
