Joshua Michael Da Silva (born (1998-06-19)19 June 1998) is a Trinidadian professional cricket right-handed batter and wicket-keeper. He has represented Trinidad and Tobago since 2018 and the West Indies since 2020. As of 2026, he is signed with the Trinbago Knight Riders in the Caribbean Premier League (CPL).
