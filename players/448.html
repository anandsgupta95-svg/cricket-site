Adeel Malik (born 17 October 1985) is a Pakistani cricketer. Born in Sialkot, Pakistan, Malik bowls right-arm leg break and bats right handed. He represented Essex in the 2015 season and has previously represented Sialkot Stallions and Pakistan International Airlines. His brother Shoaib Malik is a former captain of the Pakistan cricket team.
