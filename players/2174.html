Andrew William Mathieson (born 10 October 1989) is a New Zealand former international and first-class cricketer. He made his One Day International (ODI) debut against England on 20 June 2015. With his first delivery, he removed Jason Roy for his first ODI wicket, and finished with figures of 1 for 40 in 4 overs for the match.
