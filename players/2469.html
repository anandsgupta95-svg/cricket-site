Bella Grace Armstrong (born 16 November 1999) is a New Zealand cricketer who currently plays for Auckland. She plays as a right-handed batter and right-arm medium bowler. She has also played for Irish side Dragons. She made her international debut in October 2023, in a Twenty20 International for New Zealand against South Africa.
