Jishan Alam (Bengali: জিসান আলম; born 10 November 2004) is a Bangladeshi cricketer. A right-handed batsman and right-arm offbreak bowler, Alam plays as a batting allrounder. He has represented Bangladesh at various youth levels, including the Under-19 and Bangladesh A teams.
