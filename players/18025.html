Charith Jayampathi (born 1 February 1991) is a Sri Lankan first-class cricketer. He plays for Tamil Union Cricket and Athletic Club. Jayampathi represented Sri Lanka in the 2010 Under-19 Cricket World Cup held in New Zealand. His best performance came during the U-19 tour of Australia in 2009, where he secured nine wickets, becoming the leading Sri Lankan wicket taker in the tour. Jayampathi started his first-class cricket career with the Seeduwa Raddoluwa Cricket Club during the 2008–09 season.
