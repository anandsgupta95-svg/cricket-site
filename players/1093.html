Amy Louise Edgar (born 27 December 1997) is an Australian cricketer who plays as a right-handed batter and right-arm off break bowler for Western Australia in the Women's National Cricket League (WNCL) and Perth Scorchers in the Women's Big Bash League (WBBL). Edgar made her maiden WNCL half-century on 1 February 2019, scoring 67 in a 78-run loss to Tasmania. She spent part of the 2019–20 WBBL with the Perth Scorchers as a local replacement player but did not make an appearance. After a season away from the Scorchers, she made her debut for the side against Sydney Sixers in the 2021–22 WBBL
