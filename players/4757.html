Ewen John Chatfield (born 3 July 1950) is a former New Zealand cricketer. A medium-pace bowler, though Chatfield played 43 Tests and 114 One Day Internationals for his country. He is also remembered for having been hit in the head by a ball while batting, causing him to collapse and need resuscitation. With the ball his chief weapon was accuracy, giving him economic bowling figures.
