Craig Murray Spearman (born 4 July 1972) is an English-New Zealand former cricketer who played 19 Tests and 51 One Day Internationals for New Zealand from 1995 to 2001. Spearman was a member of the New Zealand team that won the 2000 ICC KnockOut Trophy. He took his education from Kelston Boys High School, Auckland and then to Massey University, New Zealand.
