Asad Shafiq (Urdu: اسد شفیق; born 28 January 1986) is a Pakistani cricket coach and former cricketer who played for the Pakistan national cricket team between 2010 and 2020. He is a member of the Men's National Selection Committee of the Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB). In August 2018, he was one of 33 players to be awarded a central contract for the 2018–19 season by the PCB. In September 2019, he was named in Sindh's squad for the 2019–20 Quaid-e-Azam Trophy tournament.
