Gregory Robert Hay (born 14 July 1984) is a New Zealand cricketer who played for Central Districts (officially known as the Central Stags) and for Nelson in the Hawke Cup competition until his retirement in March 2024. He is one of just two men to have lifted the Plunket Shield twice as the Central Stags captain, and retired as one of only two Central Stags players to have scored more than 7,000 first-class runs for his team.
