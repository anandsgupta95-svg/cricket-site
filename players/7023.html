Jamie Ardley Atkins (born 20 May 2002) is a former English cricketer. He made his first-class debut on 29 April 2021, for Sussex in the 2021 County Championship. Prior to his first-class debut, Atkins has played in Sussex's under-15 and under-17 teams. At the end of the 2023 season, Atkins retired from cricket to pursue a career outside of the professional game.
