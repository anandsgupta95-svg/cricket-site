Raymond Lawrence Toole (born 30 October 1997) is a New Zealand cricketer, who is a left-arm medium bowler. He plays for Central Districts in domestic cricket. He made his first-class debut on 30 October 2019, for Central Districts in the 2019–20 Plunket Shield season. He made his List A debut on 17 November 2019, for Central Districts in the 2019–20 Ford Trophy. He made his Twenty20 debut for Central Districts, on 27 December 2022, against Wellington in the 2022–23 Men's Super Smash. On 4 April 2023, he took his best bowling figures of 7 for 57 against Auckland, helping his team to win the match by 188 runs and clinch their 12th Plunket Shield title.
