Abdul Rahman Rahmani (born 22 November 2001), popularly known as Abdul Rahman, is an Afghan Pashayi cricketer. He made his first-class debut on 16 April 2019 for Kabul Region in the 2019 Ahmad Shah Abdali 4-day Tournament. He made his Twenty20 debut on 13 October 2019 for Kabul Eagles in the 2019 Shpageeza Cricket League. He was a member of Afghanistan's squad for the 2020 Under-19 Cricket World Cup. He made his List A debut on 17 October 2021 for Band-e-Amir Region in the 2021 Ghazi Amanullah Khan Regional One Day Tournament.
