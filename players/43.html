Aakash Chand (born 18 December 2004) is a Nepalese cricketer who plays as a bowler for the Nepal national cricket team. Aakash Chand was born in Bheemdatt, Kanchanpur, Nepal. He is a right-handed batsman and a right-arm medium-pace bowler who primarily plays as a bowler for the Nepal national cricket team.
