Hasan Mahmud (Bengali: হাসান মাহমুদ; born 12 October 1999) is a Bangladeshi cricket right-arm fast-medium bowler. He has represented the Bangladesh national team since 2020, and Chittagong Division since 2017. As of 2026, Mahmud is signed with Noakhali Express in the Bangladesh Premier League (BPL) and Kent in English county cricket.
