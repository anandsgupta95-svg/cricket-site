Jo Angel (born 22 April 1968) is an Australian former cricketer who played in four Test matches and three One Day Internationals from 1993 to 1995. A giant fast bowler standing 6 feet 6 inches (198 cm) tall, Angel took 485 first-class wickets, including 445 in Australian domestic cricket for Western Australia.
