Anshuman Rath (born 5 November 1997) is an Indian-born Hong Kong cricketer who previously captained the Hong Kong national cricket team. In September 2019, he retired from the Hong Kong national team to pursue a career in India. In August 2021, he became eligible to play as a local player in India as an Indian passport holder, and represented Odisha from the 2021–22 Indian domestic cricket season. Rath returned to Hong Kong's squad for the Hong Kong Quadrangular Series in March 2023.
