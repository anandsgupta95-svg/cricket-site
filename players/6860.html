Imtiaz Hossain (born 24 February 1985) is a first-class and List A cricketer from Bangladesh. A right-handed batsman and off-break bowler, he made his debut for Sylhet Division in 2001–02. His highest first-class score is 154 for Sylhet Division against Rajshahi Division in 2015–16. His best bowling figures, 3 for 50, came against Khulna Division in 2001–02. His highest List A score is 124 for Sylhet Division against Barisal Division and his best List A bowling figures are 4 for 22 against Chittagong Division in 2001–02.
