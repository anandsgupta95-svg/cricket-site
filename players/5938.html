Gurinder Singh (born 9 July 1992) is an Indian cricketer. He made his first-class debut for Tripura in the 2016–17 Ranji Trophy on 6 October 2016. He was the joint-leading wicket-taker for Meghalaya in the 2018–19 Vijay Hazare Trophy, with fourteen dismissals in eight matches. He was again Meghalaya's highest wicket-taker in the 2018–19 Ranji Trophy, with 53 dismissals in eight matches.
