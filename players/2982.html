Caoimhe Bray ( KEE-və BRAY; born 23 September 2009) is an Australian cricketer who plays for the Sydney Sixers in the Women's Big Bash League. An all-rounder, she plays as a right-handed batter and right-arm fast-medium bowler and has drawn comparison to Sixers teammate Ellyse Perry. Besides cricket, Bray also plays soccer for the Junior Matildas as a goalkeeper.
