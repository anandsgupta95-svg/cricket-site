Robert Julian Turner (born 25 November 1967) is an English former first-class cricketer. A right-handed wicket-keeper-batsman, Turner started his career in 1988 with Cambridge University, whom he captained. He began playing for Somerset County Cricket Club in 1991 and was their first-choice wicket-keeper until his retirement in 2005. He took 753 first-class dismissals in his 250-game career. Turner scored over 1,000 runs in both 1997 and 1999. Born at Malvern in 1967, Turner was educated at Millfield School before going up to Magdalene College, Cambridge.
