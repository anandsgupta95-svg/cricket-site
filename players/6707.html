Iftikhar Ahmed (Urdu & Pashto: افتخار احمد; born 3 September 1990) is a Pakistani international cricketer. Who plays for Peshawar Zalmi in the Pakistan Super League, he has previously played for Karachi Kings, Islamabad United, Quetta Gladiators and Multan Sultans. He made his international debut for the Pakistan cricket team in November 2015.
