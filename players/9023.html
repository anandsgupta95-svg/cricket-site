Kelly Sarah Anderson (née Hill; born 4 February 1983) is a New Zealand former cricketer who played as a right-arm medium bowler. She appeared in six One Day Internationals and two Twenty20 Internationals in 2011. She played domestic cricket for Northern Districts, Auckland and Canterbury.
