Inoshi Priyadharshani (born 23 March 1987) is a Sri Lankan cricketer who plays for the national women's cricket team. A right-handed batter and right-arm off-spin bowler, Priyadharshani made her One Day International (ODI) debut against Australia on 18 September 2016. She was part of Sri Lanka's squad for the 2018 ICC Women's World Twenty20 tournament in the West Indies, as well as for the 2024 ICC Women's T20 World Cup.
