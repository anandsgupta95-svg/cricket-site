Hokaito Zhimomi (born 24 September 1986) is an Indian first-class cricketer from Nagaland, who played for Assam and Nagaland. He is from Sangtamtila village of Dimapur district. He was part of Kolkata Knight Riders in the first edition of IPL, He was never part of the playing 11.
