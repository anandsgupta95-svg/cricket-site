Ayasha Rahman Shukhtara Boishakhi (Bengali: আয়শা রহমান সুখতারা বৈশাখী, Bengali pronunciation: [ae̯ʃa rɔɦman sukʰtara boi̯ʃakʰi]; born 2 February 1994) is a Bangladeshi cricketer who plays for the Bangladesh cricket team. She plays as a right-handed batter and right-arm off break bowler. Rahman was born in Khulna, Bangladesh.
