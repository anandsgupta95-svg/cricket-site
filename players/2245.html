Alexander Zak Lees (born 14 April 1993), popularly known as Alex Lees, is an English professional cricketer who plays internationally for the England Test cricket team. In domestic cricket, he represents Durham, captaining the List A and T20 teams, since March 2023. He became Durham's First Class captain before the start of the 2025 season, replacing Scott Borthwick. He previously played for Yorkshire. Lees made his Test debut in 2022, and plays as a left-handed opening batsman.
