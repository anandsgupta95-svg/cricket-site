Arshad Khan (born 20 December 1997) is an Indian cricketer who plays for Madhya Pradesh in domestic cricket and currently for Gujarat Titans in the Indian Premier League. He made his List A debut on 24 February 2021, for Madhya Pradesh in the 2020–21 Vijay Hazare Trophy. He made his T20 debut for Mumbai Indians in 2023 Indian Premier League, against Royal Challengers Bangalore on 2 April 2023. Khan was bought by the Lucknow Super Giants at the base price of 20 lakhs during the player auction for the 2024 Indian Premier League. On 13 May 2024 coming in to bat at number 9 against Delhi Capitals, Khan struck 58* off just 33 balls with 3 fours and 5 sixes along with posting figures of 1/45 nearly taking his side to a victory.
