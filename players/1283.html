Asitha Madusanka Fernando (born 31 July 1997) is a Sri Lankan cricketer who has played for the national team since 2017 across all international formats. A right arm medium-fast bowler, he has taken 80 wickets in 24 Test matches. As of May 2026, Fernando has the best career strike rate and the second best average for a Sri Lankan Test bowler. In the Lanka Premier League (LPL), he has played for the Jaffna Kings since the 2022 season, and for the Galle Gladiators in the inaugural 2020 season.
