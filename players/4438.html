David Scanlon (born 7 August 1984) is an Irish cricketer. A right-arm medium-pace bowler, Scanlon made his List A debut for North West Warriors in the 2017 Inter-Provincial Cup on 29 May 2017. He made his first-class and Twenty20 debut also for North West Warriors in the 2017 Inter-Provincial Championship on 30 May 2017, and in the 2017 Inter-Provincial Trophy on 23 June 2017, respectively. He was the leading wicket-taker in the 2018 Inter-Provincial Championship, with 19 dismissals in four matches. He took his best first-class bowling figures of 5 for 29 in his debut match in 2017.
