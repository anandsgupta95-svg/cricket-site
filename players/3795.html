Dogodo Bau (born 5 December 1994) is a cricketer from Papua New Guinea. He is a right-handed wicket-keeper batsman. He made his first-class debut for Papua New Guinea against Namibia on 16 October 2016. He made his List A debut against Namibia on 21 October 2016.
