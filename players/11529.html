Nadeem Abbasi (born 10 November 1968) is a former Pakistani cricketer who played in three Test matches in 1989. He also captained Khan Research Laboratories and Rawalpindi in domestic first-class cricket. During this tenure, he led KRL to a runners-up spot in the National One Day Cup in 2000. After retirement, he became a very successful Rawalpindi coach and regional selector, as well as working in the Pakistan National Cricket Academy and Abbottabad Region, grooming players, who later played for Pakistan.
