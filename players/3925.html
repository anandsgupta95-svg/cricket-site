Darcey Elizabeth Morris Carter (born 31 May 2005) is a Scottish cricketer who currently plays for Lancashire. She plays as a right-arm off break bowler. She made her international debut for Scotland in July 2023, in a Twenty20 International against Thailand. Ranked third among all-time run scorers in Scotland Women’s cricket, behind Kathryn Bryce and Sarah Bryce.
