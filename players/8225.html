Jake Beath Weatherald (born 4 November 1994) is an Australian cricketer. Originally from Darwin in the Northern Territory, he is an opening batsman who plays for South Australia in both first-class and one-day cricket and plays for the Hobart Hurricanes in the Big Bash League. Weatherald has also previously played domestic cricket for Tasmania.
