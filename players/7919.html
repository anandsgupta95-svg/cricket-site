Jeremy Gordon (born 20 January 1987) is a Guyanese-born international cricketer. He has played for the Canada national cricket team since 2012 as a right-arm fast bowler. Gordon was born in Guyana and represented the Guyana national cricket team before immigrating to Canada. He works professionally as a senior support analyst at Scotiabank.
