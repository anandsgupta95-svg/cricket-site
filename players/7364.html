Johan Cloete (born 21 July 1971) is a South African cricket umpire. He was on the ICC International Panel of Umpires until July 2016. As of March 2016, he had stood in 60 One Day Internationals and 23 Twenty20 Internationals. He was selected as one of the twenty umpires to stand in matches during the 2015 Cricket World Cup and officiated in three matches as an on-field umpire during the tournament. He is part of Cricket South Africa's umpire panel for first-class matches.
