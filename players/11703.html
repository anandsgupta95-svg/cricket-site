Mohammad Nazmul Hossain (born 5 October 1987) is a Bangladeshi former cricketer. He was born in Habiganj, Bangladesh. Nazmul Hossain was part of the 13-man Bangladesh squad that won the cricket tournament at the 2010 Asian Games in late November that year. They faced Afghanistan in the final and won by five wickets, securing the country's first gold medal at the Asian Games.
