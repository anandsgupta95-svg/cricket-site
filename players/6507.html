Hrithik Shokeen (born 14 August 2000) is an Indian cricketer who plays for Delhi in domestic cricket and has played for the Mumbai Indians in the Indian Premier League. He completed his schooling from Modern School (New Delhi). In November 2019, he was named in India U23's squad for the 2019 ACC Emerging Teams Asia Cup in Bangladesh. He made his List A debut for India U23, against Nepal, in the Emerging Teams Cup on 14 November 2019. In February 2022, he was bought by the Mumbai Indians in the auction for the 2022 Indian Premier League (IPL) tournament. He made his Twenty20 debut on 21 April 2022, for the Mumbai Indians in the 2022 IPL.
