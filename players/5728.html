Graeme Lourens van Buuren (born 22 August 1990) is a South African-born English cricketer who plays for Gloucestershire. In March 2022, he was appointed the captain of the team for first-class and List A matches and held the position until standing down in January 2025. Van Buuren initially qualified to play in English county cricket due to his British spouse, but was granted British citizenship in December 2021. In April 2022, he was bought by the Birmingham Phoenix for the 2022 season of The Hundred.
