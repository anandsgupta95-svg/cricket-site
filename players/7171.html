Jonathan Andrew Tattersall (born 15 December 1994) is an English cricketer who plays for Leicestershire County Cricket Club. He is a right-handed batsman who bowls leg breaks. He made his debut for his home county Yorkshire in the 2013 Yorkshire Bank 40 against Glamorgan. He is a former England under 19s batsman, and his fielding ability saw him fielding as substitute for the England senior side. Tattersall represented England under 19s at the 2014 Under-19 Cricket World Cup. Tattersall was released by Yorkshire in September 2015, but then recalled. He made his first-class debut on 20 June 2018, for Yorkshire in the 2018 County Championship. He made his Twenty20 debut for Yorkshire in the 2018 t20 Blast on 5 July 2018. In August 2025, it was announced that Tattersall had agreed a three-year contract to join Leicestershire from 1 November that year.
