Haider Ali (Urdu, Punjabi: حیدر علی; born 2 October 2000) is a Pakistani right handed cricketer. He made his first-class debut in September 2019. He made his international debut for the Pakistan cricket team on 1 September 2020. Due to his batting style, his compact defence technique, his off-side play and his pull shots, he has been compared to Indian cricketer Rohit Sharma. He himself considers Sharma to be his role model.
