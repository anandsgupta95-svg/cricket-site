Glen Keith Bruk-Jackson (born 25 April 1969) is a former Zimbabwean cricketer. A right-handed batsman, Bruk-Jackson played in two Test matches and one One Day International for Zimbabwe, all during their tour of Pakistan in December 1993. He played domestically for Mashonaland Country Districts and later Mashonaland.
