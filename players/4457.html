Dhawal Sunil Kulkarni (born 10 December 1988) is an Indian former cricketer. He was a right arm medium-pace bowler and right-handed lower order batsman. In first-class cricket, he played for Mumbai and in the Indian Premier League (IPL), he appeared for Mumbai Indians, Rajasthan Royals and Gujarat Lions. Based on his consistent performance in the domestic season and the IPL, he was selected for the Indian Test team for the away series against New Zealand in 2009. However, he was not selected to play in any of the three Test matches of the series.
