Benjamin James Pomare (born 15 September 2000) is a New Zealand cricketer who represents Northern Districts in domestic competitions. His primary playing role is wicket-keeper. Pomare was born in Tauranga in the Bay of Plenty, attended Tauranga Boys' College and represents the Mount Maunganui cricket club. He made his first-class debut against Wellington in the 2021–22 Plunket Shield season. He made his List A debut in the 2023–24 season, and his Twenty20 debut the season after. Pomare also represented New Zealand at the 2020 Under-19 Cricket World Cup.
