Akshdeep Deependra Nath (born 10 May 1993) is an Indian-born Emirati cricketer who plays for the United Arab Emirates. He is a right-handed batsman and occasional right arm medium pace bowler. He was the vice-captain of the India Under-19 cricket team that won the 2012 ICC Under-19 Cricket World Cup in Australia. He also played for Uttar Pradesh in Indian domestic cricket. He was the leading run-scorer for Uttar Pradesh in the 2017–18 Ranji Trophy, with 387 runs in five matches. He was also the leading run-scorer for Uttar Pradesh in the 2018–19 Vijay Hazare Trophy, with 293 runs in six matches. In August 2019, he was named in the India Green team's squad for the 2019–20 Duleep Trophy.
