Donovan Grobbelaar (born 30 July 1983) is a New Zealand former cricketer who played for Auckland. He made his List A debut in February 2013 in the 2012–13 Ford Trophy. Beginning in the 2021/22 season Grobbelaar became the Performance and Talent Coach of the Auckland Hearts.
