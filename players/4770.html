Edward James Nuttall (born 15 July 1993) is a fast left-arm bowler. He plays cricket for the Canterbury Kings and represented New Zealand in the 2011 ICC Under-19 Cricket World Cup. In June 2018, he was awarded a contract with Canterbury for the 2018–19 season. In June 2020, he was offered a contract by Canterbury ahead of the 2020–21 domestic cricket season. Edward is the son of Andrew Nuttall, who also played first class cricket for Canterbury.
