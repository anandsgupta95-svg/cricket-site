Buddhi Bahadhur Pradhan (Nepali: बुद्धि प्रधान) is an international cricket umpire from Nepal. He was one of the seventeen on-field umpires for the 2018 Under-19 Cricket World Cup. He has also officiated as an on-field umpire in the 2022 Under-19 Cricket World Cup.
