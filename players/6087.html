Md Hasanuzzaman (born 1 June 1991) is a Bangladeshi cricketer. He made his Twenty20 (T20) debut on 12 November 2016 for Khulna Titans in the 2016–17 Bangladesh Premier League. He made his first-class debut for Khulna Division in the 2016–17 National Cricket League on 3 January 2017. A right-handed opening batsman and part-time off spin bowler, Hasanuzzaman has played Bangladeshi domestic cricket for Khulna Titans and the Khulna Division.
