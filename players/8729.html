Kieran Elliott (born 12 September 1995) is an Australian cricketer. He made his first-class cricket debut for Tasmania against Western Australia on 16 February 2024. In February 2025, he took his first five-wicket haul and ten-wicket haul in first-class cricket against Victoria. Elliott moved to Western Australia ahead of the 2026-27 summer.
