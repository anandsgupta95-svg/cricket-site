Aphisara Suwanchonrathi (Thai:อภิสรา สุวรรณชลธี, born 29 March 2007) is a Thai cricketer. She was a part of Thai team in 2022 Women's Asia Cup and made her T20I debut against Pakistan. In January 2024, she was selected to play for Thailand in ACC Women's Premier Cup. Suwanchonrathi was part of the Thailand squad for the 2025 Women's Cricket World Cup Qualifier in Pakistan in April 2025.
