K. V. Sasikanth (born 17 July 1995) is an Indian cricketer who plays for Andhra Pradesh. He made his first-class debut on 22 October 2015 in the 2015–16 Ranji Trophy. He made his List A debut on 10 December 2015 in the 2015–16 Vijay Hazare Trophy. Sasikanth was the joint leading wicket-taker for Andhra in the 2018–19 Ranji Trophy, with 17 dismissals in four matches. He took his best first-class bowling figures in that season, taking 6 for 18 and helping to dismiss Madhya Pradesh for 35.
