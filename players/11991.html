Neil Kruger (born 15 August 1981 in Cape Town, South Africa) is a first-class cricketer, who made his debut for Oxford University cricket team in 2008, scoring 172 in the Varsity Match. Since June 2011, Kruger has also represented the Netherlands. A Rhodes Scholar and orthopedic surgeon, he studied at Green Templeton College at the University of Oxford. He held a scholarship for sporting achievement from Green Templeton College. Kruger also played varsity golf.
