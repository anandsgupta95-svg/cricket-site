Jatin Wadhwan (born 15 September 1994) is an Indian cricketer. He made his Twenty20 debut for Jammu & Kashmir in the 2015–16 Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy on 10 January 2016. He later made his List A debut for the team in the 2017–18 Vijay Hazare Trophy on 8 February 2018, followed by his first-class debut on 17 February 2022 in the 2021–22 Ranji Trophy.
