Azam Khan (Sindhi and Urdu: اعظم خان; born 10 August 1998) is a Pakistani international cricketer who plays domestically for Karachi Kings and Sindh. He is the son of former Pakistan wicket-keeper Moin Khan. Known as a power-hitter, he considers West Indies player Chris Gayle to be his batting role-model.
