Aneurin Henry Thomas Donald (an-EYE-rin; born 20 December 1996) is a Welsh cricketer who has played first-class cricket for Glamorgan, Hampshire and Derbyshire as a right-handed batsman and right-arm off spin bowler. In December 2015 he was named in England's squad for the 2016 Under-19 Cricket World Cup.
