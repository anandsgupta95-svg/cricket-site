Kaki Nitish Kumar Reddy (Telugu: [nitiːʃ kumaːɾ ɾeɖːiː]; born 26 May 2003) is an Indian international cricketer. He plays for the India national team in all three formats: as an all-rounder, who bats right-handed and bowls right-arm medium fast. He represents Andhra in domestic cricket and Sunrisers Hyderabad in the Indian Premier League.
