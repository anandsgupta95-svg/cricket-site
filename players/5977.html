Qazi Habibul Bashar (Bengali: কাজী হাবিবুল বাশার; born 17 August 1972) is a Bangladeshi cricket administrator and former cricketer who played as a right-handed batter for the Bangladesh national team from 1995 to 2008. He has served as the team's chief selector since March 2026. By the time of his retirement, he held several national batting records in both Test and One Day International (ODI) formats. He served as the captain of the national team from 2004 to 2007.
