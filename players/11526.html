Nabil Samad (born 9 October 1986, in Dhaka) is a Bangladeshi cricketer who plays first-class cricket for Sylhet Division. A spin bowler, he represented Bangladesh in the 2006 U-19 Cricket World Cup in Sri Lanka. Nabil was bought by Sylhet Royals for $20,000 and proved effective from his first game by bowling flat for low economy rates. He joined Chittagong Division for the 2015–16 Bangladeshi cricket season. In October 2018, he was named in the squad for the Sylhet Sixers team, following the draft for the 2018–19 Bangladesh Premier League.
