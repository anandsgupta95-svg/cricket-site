David Odhiambo (born 27 April 1976) is a Kenyan cricket umpire. As of April 2023, he has stood in nine One Day International (ODIs), 31 Twenty20 Internationals (T20Is), one women's ODI and four women's T20I matches. He was one of the seventeen on-field umpires for the 2018 Under-19 Cricket World Cup, held in New Zealand. He was named as one of the eight on-field umpires for the 2019 ICC World Cricket League Division Two tournament in Namibia.
