Champaka Priyadarshana Hewage Ramanayake (born 8 January 1965), or Champaka Ramanayake, is a Sri Lankan former cricketer who played in 18 Test matches and 62 One Day Internationals from 1986 to 1995. Champaka is a cricket coach. He worked as the national fast bowling coach for Sri Lanka Cricket board for 15 years and Bangladesh Cricket Board for two years. One of Champaka's greatest achievements is discovering Lasith Malinga at the age of 16 and coaching him. He is the current high performance fast bowling coach of the Bangladesh national side.
