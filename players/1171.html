Anna Louise Kerrison (born 10 September 1998) is an Irish cricketer who plays for Scorchers and Ireland. In October 2014, at the age of 16, she became the first female cricketer to train at the Darren Lehmann Cricket Academy in Australia. In May 2019, she was added to Ireland's Women's Twenty20 International (WT20I) squad for their series against the West Indies. She made her WT20I debut for Ireland against the West Indies on 28 May 2019.
