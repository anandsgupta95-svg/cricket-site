Craig James Robert Jennings (born 13 November 1984) is an English cricketer. Jennings is a right-handed batsman who bowls right-arm fast-medium. He was born at Rugeley, Staffordshire. Jennings played a single List A match for the Northamptonshire Cricket Board against the Leicestershire Cricket Board in the 1st round of the 2002 Cheltenham & Gloucester Trophy which was played in 2001. Jennings made a single first-class appearance for Northamptonshire against Worcestershire in the 2004 County Championship. In his only first-class match he scored 6 runs and took a single wicket. During the same season he also represented the county in 2 List-A matches against the touring New Zealanders and Gloucestershire in the totesport League. In the 3 List-A matches he played, he scored 6 runs at a batting average of 3.00, with a high score of 3. With the ball he took 2 wickets at a bowling average of 64.00, with best figures of 1/29. Also during the 2004 season he played a single Twenty20 match for the county against Worcestershire in the 2004 Twenty20 Cup.
