Chamindu Wickramasinghe (Sinhala: චමිඳු වික්‍රමසිංහ, [tʃamiⁿd̪u ʋikrəməsiŋɦə]; born 6 September 2002) is a professional Sri Lankan cricketer who plays Twenty20 Internationals for the national team and all formats for Sinhalese Sports Club and Dambulla Sixers in domestic cricket. He plays as an all-rounder.
