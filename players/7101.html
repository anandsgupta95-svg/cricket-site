Jamaluddin Ahmed (born 5 January 1977) is a Bangladeshi former cricketer who played in one One Day International in 2003. A middle-order batsman and off-spin bowler, he played first-class cricket for Khulna Division from 2001 to 2008. His first ever century (121) helped Khulna win the final of the National Cricket League in 2002–03, and won him the man of the match award. He took his best innings and match bowling figures against Barisal Division in 2005-06: 8 for 67 and 3 for 45.
