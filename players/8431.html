Kaynat Hafeez (born 17 June 1996) is a Pakistani cricket all-rounder, who plays as a right-handed batter and right-arm off break bowler. In November 2019, she was named in Pakistan's squads for their series against England in Malaysia. She made her Women's One Day International (WODI) for Pakistan, against England, on 14 December 2019. In June 2021, she was named in Pakistan's squad for their series against the West Indies. She has played domestic cricket for Lahore, the Higher Education Commission and the State Bank of Pakistan.
