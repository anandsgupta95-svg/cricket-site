Abhimanyu Easwaran (Tamil: [abimaɳjuː iːsʋaɾan]) is an Indian cricketer who plays for Bengal. He is a right-handed opening batter. He also captained the India A, India B in Duleep Trophy and Bengal in Ranji team.
