Hendrik-Jan Christiaan Mol (born 29 March 1977) is a Dutch former cricketer. He is a left-handed batsman and a left-arm medium-pace bowler, with an action comparable to Wasim Akram's. He is also a quick infielder. His left-arm protoge is Mart Spruit. Experts claim he could surpass Hendrik-Jan Mol one day.
