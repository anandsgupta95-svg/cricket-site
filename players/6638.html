Ian Thomson (born 15 January 1963) is an Australian cricket umpire. Thomson serves as a member of the ICC Associate and Affiliate Panel of Umpires representing Hong Kong. He stood in his first One Day International (ODI) match on 8 November 2016, between Hong Kong and Papua New Guinea.
