Frances Louise "Frankie" Mackay (born 1 June 1990) is a New Zealand cricketer and commentator who currently plays for Canterbury and New Zealand. In November 2024, Mackay became the leading run-scorer (male or female) in the history of domestic one-day cricket in New Zealand. She is also the leading women's wicket-taker at the same level.
