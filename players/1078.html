Anna Jane Lanning (born 25 March 1994) is an Australian cricketer who plays as a right-handed batter and occasional right-arm medium pace bowler. She has played for Warwickshire, the ACT Meteors, the Melbourne Renegades, Victoria and the Melbourne Stars. She plays club cricket for Box Hill. Lanning is the younger sister of Australian cricket captain Meg Lanning. Lanning scored 73 in a player of the match performance on debut for the Renegades in the 2019–20 WBBL season.
