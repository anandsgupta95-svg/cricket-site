Hashim Mahomed Amla (born 31 March 1983) is a South African former cricketer who played for the national team from 2004 to 2019. A right-handed batter, he is South Africa's second-highest run-scorer in Tests and third-highest in One Day Internationals (ODIs), with 55 international centuries across both formats. He also played Twenty20 Internationals (T20s). Amla served as the captain in several international matches from early to mid-2010s. With a professional career spanning from 1999/2000 to 2022, he represented several domestic and franchise cricket teams around the world.
