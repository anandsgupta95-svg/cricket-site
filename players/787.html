Malik Ahsan Ahmad Jamil (born 29 August 1989) is a Dutch cricketer. He is originally from Gujrat, Punjab, now in Pakistan. He played for the Netherlands in the 2014 ICC World Twenty20 tournament. He was the leading wicket-taker along with Imran Tahir in the 2014 ICC World Twenty20
