Imliwati Lemtur (born 25 December 1991) is an Indian cricketer. He plays for Nagaland as a bowling All-rounder. He made his List A debut for Nagaland in the 2018–19 Vijay Hazare Trophy on 19 September 2018. He was the leading wicket-taker for Nagaland in the 2018–19 Vijay Hazare Trophy, with ten dismissals in seven matches. He made his first-class debut for Nagaland in the 2018–19 Ranji Trophy on 1 November 2018. He made his Twenty20 debut for Nagaland in the 2018–19 Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy on 21 February 2019.
