Jacqueline Williams (born 4 March 1976) is a Jamaican cricket umpire. She has officiated in the 2015 ICC Women's World Twenty20 Qualifier tournament. She became the first female umpire to stand in the domestic 50-over competition in the West Indies, when she was one of the on-field umpires in the match between Trinidad & Tobago and ICC Americas in the 2015–16 Regional Super50 tournament. On 14 December 2023, she became the first female umpire from the West Indies to stand in a men's T20I involving two Full-Member teams.
