Hasan Ali (Punjabi and Urdu: حسن علی; born 2 July 1994) is a Pakistani international cricketer. He plays for the Pakistan national team in all formats as a right-arm medium fast bowler. He is the quickest bowler for Pakistan to take 50 wickets in ODIs. Hasan Ali was a member of the Pakistan team that won the 2017 ICC Champions Trophy.
