Jintimani Kalita (born 25 December 2003) is an Indian cricketer who plays for Assam and Mumbai Indians. She plays as a right-arm medium bowler and left-handed batter. At the inaugural Women's Premier League auction held in February 2023, Kalita was acquired by the Mumbai Indians for INR 10 lakhs.
