Geoff Joshua (born 12 March 1970) is an Australian cricket umpire. He has stood in matches in the Big Bash League tournament and in women's One Day International matches in the 2014–16 ICC Women's Championship. In November 2017, along with Gerard Abood, he was one of the on-field umpires for the Women's Ashes Test match between Australia and England.
