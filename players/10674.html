Mahela Lakmal Udawatte known as Mahela Udawatte (Sinhala: මහේල උඩවත්ත; born 19 July 1986) is a professional Sri Lankan International cricketer. He played as an opening batsman in his debut series in 2008, and shifted to become a middle order batsman for his comeback in 2017. He played his first Test match against the West Indies in 2018, 10 years after his limited overs debut.
