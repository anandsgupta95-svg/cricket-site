Jack Alexander Brooks (born 4 June 1984) is an English professional cricket player, currently playing for Somerset after successful spells at Oxfordshire in the Minor Counties, and Northamptonshire and Yorkshire in first-class cricket. He is predominantly a right arm medium fast bowler who can also bat.

Insights

  • Favourite victim: has dismissed Tom Abell 7 times.
  • A four-wicket haul or better in 32 of 158 First-class bowling innings (one every 4.9).
  • Much more effective against right-handers: bowling average 23 versus 44.5 against left-handers.
  • Loves bowling to Northamptonshire: 19 wickets at 15.1 in 6 First-class innings (career average 27.8).
  • Most frequent team-mate: Adam Lyth, together in 75 matches.
  • Usually bats at No. 11 in first-class matches (56 of 121 innings).
