Bevon John Jacobs (born 6 May 2002) is a New Zealand cricketer, who is a right-handed batsman. He currently represents the Auckland cricket team, and has previously played for Canterbury.

Insights

  • Keeps the strike rotating: only 32% dot balls faced in limited-overs cricket.
  • Boundary hitter: a four or six every 5.3 balls in limited-overs cricket.
  • Death-overs specialist: 382 runs off 205 balls (SR 186) in the death overs (16–20) in domestic T20s.
  • Far stronger against spin: averages 52.6 (SR 105) versus 34.7 (SR 98) against pace, across all formats.
  • Tempo shifts by phase in domestic T20s: strikes at 186 in the death overs (16–20) but 134 in the middle overs (7–15).
  • Usually bats at No. 4 in domestic T20s (11 of 32 innings).
