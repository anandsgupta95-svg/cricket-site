Qanita Jalil (born 21 March 1978) is a Pakistani former cricketer who played as a right-arm fast-medium bowler and right-handed batter. She appeared in 66 One Day Internationals and 51 Twenty20 Internationals for Pakistan between 2005 and 2015. She played domestic cricket for Peshawar, Abbottabad and Zarai Taraqiati Bank Limited. She was a member of the gold-medal-winning Pakistan team at the 2014 Asian Games. She is the sister of Pakistani First class cricketer Nasir Jalil.
