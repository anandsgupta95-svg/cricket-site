Mohsin Kamal (born 16 June 1963) is a former Pakistani cricketer who played in nine Test matches and 19 One Day Internationals from 1984 to 1994. Kamal was appointed coach of the Bangladesh national cricket team in 2002. He was sacked in March 2003 shortly before the end of his one-year contract, following the team's poor performance at the 2003 Cricket World Cup in South Africa.
