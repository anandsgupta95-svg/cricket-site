Farrukh Zaman (born 2 April 1956) is a Pakistani former cricketer who played in one Test match in 1976. A slow left-arm orthodox bowler, he played first-class cricket in Pakistan from 1971–72 to 1995–96. In 1976–77, Zaman claimed seven wickets for the NWFP Chief Minister's XI against the touring New Zealanders. He was selected for the second Test in the series, but was given only 10 overs in the match: six overs in the first innings (0 for 8) and four in the second (0 for 7).
