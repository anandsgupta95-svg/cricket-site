Michael Walter William Selvey (born 25 April 1948) is an English former Test and county cricketer, and now a cricket writer and commentator. A fast-medium bowler, Selvey played in three Tests for England in 1976 and 1977. He played county cricket for Surrey, Middlesex and Glamorgan. He served as the 25th President of Middlesex (2019–2023).
