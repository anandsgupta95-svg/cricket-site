Benjamin Wentworth Munro Mike (born 24 August 1998) is an English cricketer. He made his List A debut for Leicestershire against India A in a tri-series warm-up match on 19 June 2018. He made his first-class debut for Leicestershire in the 2018 County Championship on 4 September 2018. He made his Twenty20 debut on 1 May 2019, for Leicestershire against the touring Pakistan team. Mike signed for Yorkshire ahead of the 2023 season
