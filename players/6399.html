Hayleigh Maree Brennan (born 5 March 1999) is an Australian cricketer. Born in Dandenong, a suburb of Melbourne, Victoria, she is a right-arm medium bowler. She has represented Victoria (2016/17–2017/18) and Middlesex (2018) in List A cricket. In the Women's Big Bash League (WBBL), she played for the Melbourne Stars during the 2017–18 season and the Perth Scorchers during the 2018–19 season. Brennan played for Australia A against England Academy Women in April 2016.
