Jamal Tana Todd (born 27 March 2004) is a New Zealand cricketer who has played for Otago since the 2023–24 season. Born in Auckland on 27 March 2004, Todd was educated at Auckland Grammar School. He moved to Dunedin in 2022 to study sports management and Māori studies at the University of Otago. In his second first-class match, in the 2023–24 Plunket Shield, he scored 30 and 64 (the top score of the innings) for Otago against Canterbury. In the provincial A T20 tournament in Taupō in November 2024, Todd scored two centuries for Otago A in two matches on the same day: 117 off 53 balls against Canterbury A, then 108 from 42 balls against Auckland A. He won the player of the match award when he scored 51 not out off 28 balls to take Otago to victory by eight wickets over Central Districts in the 2024–25 Super Smash. Of Māori descent, Todd affiliates to Ngāti Whātua. In 2021, he was named in the Māori Secondary Schoolboys cricket team. Todd's younger sister Anika plays for Auckland.
