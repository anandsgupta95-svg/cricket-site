Karl Hurter (born 12 February 1964) is a South African former cricket umpire. Hurter umpired in five international games. He made his One Day International (ODI) umpiring debut on 27 March 2002, when the visiting Australia played South Africa. Hurter stood in four ODIs between 2002 and 2006 and officiated in his only Twenty20 International in 2006.
