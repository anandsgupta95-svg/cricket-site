Andrew John Neal (born 11 October 1999) is an English cricketer. He made his first-class debut on 26 March 2019, for Leeds/Bradford MCCU against Derbyshire, as part of the Marylebone Cricket Club University fixtures. Neal joined Hampshire on a short-term basis in July 2025. He played 11 One-Day Cup matches, finishing as the team's leading wicket-taker in the competition with 14 scalps and going on to sign a two-year contract with the club in December that year.
