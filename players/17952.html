Hiruka Fernando (born 30 September 1976) is a former Sri Lankan cricketer. She played in 60 Women's One Day International (WODIS) and 4 Women's Twenty20 International (WT20Is) for Sri Lanka. She was the first woman to score 1,000 runs in ODIs for Sri Lanka. Her sister Rose also played cricket for Sri Lanka. She played domestic cricket for Colts Cricket Club. In October 2008, the Fernando sister called up for the West Indies series. In October 2010, she retired from International cricket.
