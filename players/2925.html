Craig Andrew Philipson (born 18 November 1982) is an Australian cricketer who played for Queensland in Australian domestic cricket as an attacking right-handed batsman. Philipson scored 110 on his first class debut, against Tasmania. In 2004–05 he had his best season to date with 447 Pura Cup runs at 37.25. He is a former Australia Under-19 player and was part of the side that won the 2002 World Cup.
