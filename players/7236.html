Joseph Benjamin Cracknell (born 16 March 2000) is an English cricketer. He made his Twenty20 debut on 21 June 2019, for Middlesex against Leinster Lightning. He made his first-class debut on 11 July 2021, for Middlesex in the 2021 County Championship. He made his List A debut on 25 July 2021, for Middlesex in the 2021 Royal London One-Day Cup. Cracknell grew up playing at North Middlesex Cricket Club. He later represented Durham University while completing a degree in Sports Science.
