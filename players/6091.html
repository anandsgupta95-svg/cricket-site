Haseeb Amjad (born 11 November 1987) is a Hong Kong cricketer. He played for Hong Kong in the 2014 ICC World Twenty20 tournament. He made his One Day International (ODI) debut against Afghanistan in the 2014 ACC Premier League on 1 May 2014. Amjad played seven ODIs and 18 Twenty20 Internationals for the national side. In October 2018, he was charged with five offences under the ICC Anti-Corruption Code. In August 2019, the ICC banned him for five years from all forms of cricket for spot fixing. The majority of the offences related to matches played by Hong Kong against Canada and Scotland during the 2014 Cricket World Cup Qualifier tournament in New Zealand.
