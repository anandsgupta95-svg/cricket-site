Mohammad Irfan (born 15 May 1995) is a former Pakistani first-class cricketer who played for Water and Power Development Authority and had represented Lahore Qalandars, Karachi Kings and Quetta Gladiators in the PSL. In April 2018, he was named in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's squad for the 2018 Pakistan Cup. As of 2020, he was living in Sydney, Australia where he played for Western Suburbs as he awaited Australian citizenship.
