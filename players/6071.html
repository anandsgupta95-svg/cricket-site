Harsh Dubey (born 23 July 2002) is an Indian cricketer who plays for Vidarbha in domestic cricket and Sunrisers Hyderabad in the Indian Premier League. He made his ODI debut on 13 June 2026 against Afghanistan. With 69 wickets and 476 runs, Dubey won the award for the player of the tournament in the 2024–25 Ranji Trophy, which Vidarbha won.
