Daniel Tarric Doram (born 30 October 1997) is a Sint Maarten cricketer. He plays for the Leeward Islands cricket team in West Indian domestic cricket and has also played international cricket for the Netherlands national cricket team. He is a left-arm orthodox bowler who stands 6 feet 7 inches (2.01 m) tall.
