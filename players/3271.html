Calum John Haggett (born 30 October 1990) is an English first-class cricketer who played for Kent County Cricket Club. An all-rounder, he bowls right-arm medium pace, and bats left-handed. He made his debut for Somerset in the 2010–11 Caribbean Twenty20 and played three T20 matches but these were the only appearances he made for Somerset. Having previously represented England U19s on their 2009 tour to Bangladesh, Haggett signed for Kent ahead of the 2013 English domestic season. He was released by Kent at the end of the 2020 season after seven seasons with the team.
