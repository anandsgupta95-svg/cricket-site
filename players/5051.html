Faisal Iqbal (Urdu: فیصل اقبال; born 30 December 1981) is a Pakistani cricket coach and former cricketer who played for the Pakistan national cricket team in Tests and One Day Internationals. Previously, he played for Karachi Region, Pakistan International Airlines (PIA), Pakistan National Shipping Corporation, Sindh Dolphins and Pakistan A in first class, List A and T20 cricket. He is now a coach and also appears as an expert cricket analyst on different TV channels.
