Asif Mehmood (Urdu: آصف محمود; born 3 February 1996) is a Pakistani cricketer who plays for Sindh. and for Washington Freedom in the MLC. Mehmood made his List A debut for Hyderabad Hawks against WAPDA. He was called up to the Pakistan national under-19 cricket team in December 2014. Mehmood made his T20 debut for Sindh against Southern Punjab during the 2022–23 National T20 Cup on 7 September 2022. On 14 September 2022, Mehmood took a hat-trick against Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
