Blake Sydney Dean (born 15 September 1987) is an Australian cricketer. He was a right-handed batsman and right arm leg spin bowler until he trained himself to bat and bowl left handed after suffering a shoulder injury. He was captain/coach of the Queanbeyan District Cricket Club, playing for the ACT Comets in the Futures League and Sydney Thunder in the Big Bash League.
