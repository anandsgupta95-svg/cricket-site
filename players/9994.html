Maitipage Athula Rohitha Samarasekera (born 5 August 1961) is a Sri Lankan Australian cricket coach and former cricketer. He was a hard hitting opening batsman and a medium fast bowler, who played four Tests and 39 One Day Internationals between 1983 and 1994. Samarasekera was a member of the Sri Lankan cricket teams participated for the Cricket World Cup in 1983 and 1992.
