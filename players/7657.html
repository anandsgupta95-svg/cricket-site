Jason Jaskirat Singh Sangha (born 8 September 1999) is an Australian cricketer. He is a right-handed batsman and right-arm leg break bowler. He represents South Australia in Australian domestic cricket.

Insights

  • Happy hunting ground: 655 runs at 109.2 in 9 First-class innings at Karen Rolton Oval, Adelaide.
  • Dismissed most often by Will Sutherland – 8 times.
  • Far stronger against spin: averages 48.6 (SR 66) versus 25.8 (SR 58) against pace, across all formats.
  • Much more effective against right-handers: bowling average 30.1 versus 61.2 against left-handers.
  • Most frequent team-mate: Matthew Gilkes, together in 53 matches.
  • Usually bats at No. 4 in first-class matches (48 of 88 innings).
  • First recorded match at age 19, in 2018.
