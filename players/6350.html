Hamish John William Gardiner (born 4 January 1991) is an Australian-born Scottish cricketer. He made his One Day International (ODI) debut against Australia on 3 September 2013 at The Grange Club. Gardiner was born in Brisbane but spent time growing up in Edinburgh.
