Raj Pradhan (Nepali: राज कुमार प्रधान) (born 27 April 1973) is a former Nepalese cricketer. Raj is a right-handed batsman and a leg spin bowler. He made his debut for Nepal against UAE in July 2002. He also represented the Panchakanya Tej of the Nepal Premier League and Region no. 1 Biratnagar of the National League.
