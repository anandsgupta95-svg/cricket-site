Brydon Alexander Carse (born 31 July 1995) is an English cricketer who plays for Durham and England. Primarily a right-arm fast bowler, he also bats right handed. Born in South Africa, he is the son of Zimbabwean cricketer James Alexander Carse, who played in England for Northamptonshire. Carse regularly clocks speeds around 91 mph (146 km/h). He made his international debut for the England cricket team in July 2021.
