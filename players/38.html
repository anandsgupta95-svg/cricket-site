Abhishek Arunkumar Jhunjhunwala (born 1 December 1982) is an Indian first-class cricketer. He played for the Royal Bengal Tigers and the Indian World Team in the Indian Cricket League Twenty20 competition, before signing for the Rajasthan Royals in the Indian Premier League. In his second match for the Royals, he hit a 7 not out. In the 4th season of IPL, he played for Pune Warriors India. In the 5th season of IPL, he moved to Deccan Chargers. Abhi is now playing for Hampton Wick having moved from Richmond CC to become HW's Director of Cricket.
