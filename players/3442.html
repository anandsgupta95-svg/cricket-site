Colin Niel Ackermann (born 4 April 1991) is a Dutch-South African cricketer who plays for Durham County Cricket Club in England and the Netherlands national cricket team. He made his international debut for the Netherlands in October 2019. He served as the Leicestershire club captain (2020–2022).
