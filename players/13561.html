Roger Dill (born 5 July 1957) is an international cricket umpire from Bermuda. He became the first umpire from the ICC Associates panel to officiate in a full One Day Internationals (ODIs) in May 2006, during the triangular series played by Bermuda, Canada and Zimbabwe. He has officiated in 25 ODIs. Dill is also a sergeant in the Bermudian fire brigade.
