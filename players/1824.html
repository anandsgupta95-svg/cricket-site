Aryan Juyal (born 11 November 2001) is an Indian cricketer. He plays as right hand batter and Wicket-keeper. He represented Uttar Pradesh in domestic cricket. He made his List A debut for Uttar Pradesh in the 2017–18 Vijay Hazare Trophy on 11 February 2018. Prior to his List A debut, he was named in India National Under-19 cricket team for the 2018 Under-19 Cricket World Cup. Juyal made his International debut against South Africa in the 2018 Under-19 Cricket World Cup in Christchurch, New Zealand.
