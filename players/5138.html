Fazl-e-Akbar Durrani (born 20 October 1980) is a Pakistani cricketer. He is a right-handed batsman and a right-arm medium-fast bowler. Fazl made his Test debut in February 1998 in a match between South Africa and Pakistan. He had an early impact, taking his first Test wicket, that of Gary Kirsten, with his sixth ball after having earlier dropped a catch from him. He managed to only play four more Tests, his last in 2004. He continued to play domestic cricket in Pakistan until 2009.
