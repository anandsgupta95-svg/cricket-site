Haris Rauf (pronounced [ɦaːɾɪs ɾəuːf]; born 7 November 1993) is a Pakistani international cricketer who plays as a right-arm fast bowler for the Pakistani national team. He also plays for Lahore Qalandars in the Pakistan Super League and Northern in first class cricket. He made his international debut for the Pakistan cricket team in January 2020 against Bangladesh. He made his Twenty20 debut for the Lahore Qalandars in the 2018 Abu Dhabi T20 Trophy on 5 October 2018. In November 2018, he was selected by the Lahore Qalandars in the players' draft for the 2019 Pakistan Super League tournament. He made his Test debut against England in December 2022.
