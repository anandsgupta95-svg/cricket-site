Ruwan Senani Kalpage (born 19 February 1970) is a former Sri Lankan cricketer. He was a left-handed batsman and a right-arm offbreak bowler. He studied at St. Anthony's College, Kandy and played for the College Cricket team in 1989. His international cricket career ended in March 1999.
