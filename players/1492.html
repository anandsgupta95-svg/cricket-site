Anis Siddiqi (born 11 April 1959) is a Pakistani former cricketer and umpire. Siddiqi was a right-handed batsman who bowled leg break. He was born in Lahore, Punjab. Siddiqi played Pakistani domestic cricket for Lahore, Pakistan Universities and Punjab. He played 23 first-class matches, scoring 689 runs at a batting average of 18.13, with 3 half-centuries and a highest score of 79. With the ball, he took 13 wickets at a bowling average of 53.15, with best figures of 2 for 38. In 5 List A matches, he scored 52 runs with a highest score of 32. After his playing career, Siddiqi became an umpire. He stood in 17 first-class matches, 15 List A matches and 6 Twenty20 matches, and also officiated in 1 Under-19 One Day International. In April 2013, the Pakistan Cricket Board banned Siddiqi for three years after an inquiry into an integrity case arising from a television sting operation.
