SK Robiul Islam (Bengali: রবিউল ইসলাম; born: 20 October 1986) is Bangladeshi former cricket right arm fast-medium bowler who played eight Test and four limited overs matches for Bangladesh between 2010 and 2014. During the 2013 tour of Zimbabwe, he set the Bangladeshi record for most wickets taken by a fast bowler in a Test series, after taking 15 wickets in two matches.
