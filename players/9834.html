Mahbubul Alam Robin (born 1 December 1983) is a Bangladeshi cricketer who as of 14 August 2009 has played four Tests and four One Day Internationals (ODI). Despite having a first-class batting average of less than seven, Mahbub scored 59 from 43 deliveries in a One Day International against Zimbabwe in Bulawayo in August 2009. According to Bangladesh's coach, Champaka Ramanayake, Mahbub is "a bowler who depends heavily on rhythm".
