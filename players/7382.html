Jarrod Douglas McKay (born 8 June 2000) is a New Zealand cricketer. He made his first-class debut on 30 October 2019, for Central Districts in the 2019–20 Plunket Shield season. He made his List A debut on 1 December 2020, for Central Districts in the 2020–21 Ford Trophy. He made his Twenty20 debut on 6 January 2023, for the Otago Volts in the 2022–23 Men's Super Smash. McKay was educated at Nelson College from 2014 to 2018.
