Claire Louise Nicholas (née Thomas; born 8 September 1986) is a retired Welsh cricketer who played for Wales and Welsh Fire. She played as a right-arm off break bowler. She won two Women's Cricket Super Leagues with Western Storm, in 2017 and 2019.
