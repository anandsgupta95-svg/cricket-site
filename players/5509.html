Glenn Dominic Phillips (born (1996-12-06)6 December 1996) is a New Zealand professional cricket all-rounder who bats right-handed, bowls right-arm off-spin and occasionally keeps wicket. He represents New Zealand in international cricket, Otago in domestic cricket and the Gujarat Titans in the Indian Premier League (IPL). Having begun his domestic career with Auckland and made his international debut in 2017, Phillips has played extensively in limited-overs leagues around the world, including the Caribbean Premier League (CPL), the Lanka Premier League (LPL) and Major League Cricket (MLC), alongside The Hundred and the T20 Blast in England.
