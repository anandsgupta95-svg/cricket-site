Imran Mir (born 30 September 2001) is an Afghan cricketer. He made his first-class debut for Mis Ainak Region in the 2017–18 Ahmad Shah Abdali 4-day Tournament on 20 October 2017. He made his Twenty20 debut on 12 October 2019, for Kabul Eagles in the 2019 Shpageeza Cricket League. Mir was a member of Afghanistan's squad for the 2020 Under-19 Cricket World Cup.
