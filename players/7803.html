John Michael Davison (born 9 May 1970) is a Canadian-Australian cricket coach and former cricketer who has coached the Italian national cricket team since 2025. He captained the national side in One Day Internationals. He was a hard-hitting right-handed batsman in the top or middle order, and also bowled right-arm off break. Davison retired in March 2011, playing his last game against Australia at the 2011 World Cup.
