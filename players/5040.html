Faheem Ashraf (Punjabi: رانا فہیم اشرف ; born 16 January 1994) is a Pakistani international cricketer who represents the national side and plays first-class cricket for Faisalabad. In August 2018, he was one of 33 players to be awarded a central contract for the 2018–19 season by the Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB). Faheem Ashraf is the first Pakistani to take a hat-trick in the T20 format of the game.
