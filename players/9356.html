Lasith Embuldeniya (Sinhala: ලසිත් ඇඹුල්දෙණිය, [lasit æᵐbuldeɳijə]; born 26 October 1996) is a professional Sri Lankan cricketer who plays Test match cricket for Sri Lanka. He made his international debut for the Sri Lanka cricket team in February 2019, and plays for Nondescripts Cricket Club in domestic cricket.
