Sameer Minhas (born 2 October 2006) is a Pakistani cricketer who plays for the Pakistan national under-19 cricket team. A top-order batter from Multan, Punjab, he rose to prominence after a series of record-breaking performances in Youth One-Day Internationals, including a score of 172 against India in the final of the 2025 ACC Under-19 Asia Cup. He is the younger brother of Pakistani international cricketer Arafat Minhas.
