Danielle Rose Gibson (born 30 April 2001) is an English cricketer who currently plays for Somerset, Sunrisers Leeds. An all-rounder, she plays as a right-arm medium bowler and right-handed batter. She has previously played for Wales, Western Storm and Adelaide Strikers. She made her international debut for England in July 2023, in a T20I against Australia.
