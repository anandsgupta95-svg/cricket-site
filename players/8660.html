Khurram Chohan (born 22 February 1980) is a Pakistani-Canadian cricketer who represented the Canada national cricket team. Chohan had a career in Pakistan's domestic cricket league before he emigrated to Canada. He played for the Lahore cricket teams and also represented Pakistan at under-19 level. Chohan was included in Canada's squad for the World Cup Qualifiers in South Africa in early 2009 and proved one of their more consistent bowlers, taking 15 wickets in the course of the tournament.
