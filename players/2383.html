Blake Carlton Cullen (born 19 February 2002) is an English cricketer. He made his first-class debut on 22 August 2020, for Middlesex against Sussex, in the 2020 Bob Willis Trophy. Prior to his first-class debut, he was named in England's squad for the 2020 Under-19 Cricket World Cup. Cullen made his Twenty20 debut on 10 June 2021, for Middlesex in the 2021 T20 Blast. In July 2021, he was selected as a wildcard by the London Spirit for the 2021 season of The Hundred. He was retained for the 2022 season but was forced to withdraw due to injury.
