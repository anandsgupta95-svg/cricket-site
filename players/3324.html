Chamara Kantha Kapugedera (Sinhala: චාමර කපුගෙදර, born 24 February 1987) is a former international Sri Lankan cricketer who played all formats of the game, and was a former One Day International captain. He was a permanent member in the national team from the debut match until 2010, and finally poor performances dropped him from the squad, until his comeback in 2015. He is an alumnus of Dharmaraja College, Kandy. He was also a member of the first Twenty20 International match for Sri Lanka, and capped no. 6. Kapugedara re-entered the national team in the mid-2015 and played the role of a middle order batsmen. In December 2019, he retired from all forms of cricket.
