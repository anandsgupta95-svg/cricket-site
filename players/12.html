Azhar Bilakhia (born 31 May 1986) is an Indian first-class cricketer who plays for Baroda. He is a middle-order batsman. In 2005, he started his career with Gujarat Cricket Association (GCA) and then shifted to BCA in 2007-08. According to the Times of India, (TOI Newspaper), Baroda Cricket Association (BCA) management received a letter from the player stating his decision to quit cricket forever.
