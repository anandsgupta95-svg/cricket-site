Mohammad Elias Sunny (Bengali: ইলিয়াস সানি, born 1 January 1986) is a Bangladeshi international cricketer. Sunny made his Test cricket debut in 2011 and has subsequently played both Test and limited overs cricket for the Bangladesh national team.
