Bharti Shrikrushna Fulmali (born 11 October 1994) is an Indian cricketer who plays for the Vidarbha women's cricket team. She has been playing cricket since she was 13, making her senior debut at the age of 17. In January 2019, she was named in India Blue's team for the 2018–19 Senior Women's Challenger Trophy. In February 2019, she was named in India's Women's Twenty20 International (WT20I) squad for their series against England. She was one of two players from Vidarbha women, the other being Komal Zanzad, to be selected for the national team. She made her WT20I debut against England on 7 March 2019.
