Simon Richard George Francis (born 15 August 1978) is an English former cricketer. Francis played county cricket at first-class level as a right-arm fast-medium bowler for Hampshire, Somerset, and Nottinghamshire in a career that spanned from 1997 to 2007. While playing for Somerset in 2004, he took figures of 8 for 66 in a one-day match against Derbyshire, establishing the best individual bowling figures taking by a Somerset player in one-day cricket. This record remains as of 2026. After the end of his playing career, Francis became the director of cricket at Warwick School.
