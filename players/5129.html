Fazalhaq Farooqi (Pashto: فضل الحق فاروقي) (born 22 September 2000) is an Afghan international cricketer who plays for the national team in limited-overs cricket. Farooqi made his international debut for the Afghanistan cricket team in March 2021. In franchise leagues, he has played for Rajasthan Royals in the Indian Premier League (IPL) and Dhaka Dominators in the Bangladesh Premier League (BPL).
